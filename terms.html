4. How Init Works

4.1 Init Lounge

Init Lounge lets you meet people who are physically near you, right now:

  • Check in. When you are at a real-world place (a café, a gym, a bar, a campus, your own building), you can check in to that lounge. Check-in requires a device location fix placing you within approximately 300 metres of the venue. If you have granted only approximate location permission, you can browse nearby lounges but cannot check in.
  • Post a vibe. Your check-in includes a short "vibe card" (15–100 characters) describing what you are up for. Other people checked in to the same lounge see your card, your age range and gender, and your photo if you choose to show it. They do not see your name, username, or profile.
  • Vibe on others. You can vibe on other people's cards. A vibe is one-way and private — the other person is not told.
  • Match. If two people vibe each other's cards in the same lounge, a match is formed and a private chat opens for both. Non-mutual vibes are never revealed.
  • Sessions end. Your check-in ends when you check out, log out, move roughly 500 metres or more from the venue, or after a maximum of 3 hours. When a session ends, your vibe cards for that session are removed.

Init Lounge is available only where you have granted the App location permission. Denying location permission disables Init Lounge; the rest of the App continues to work.

4.2 Private Interest Matching

Your interest expressions are private and invisible to others unless the interest is mutual. This is a fundamental design principle of Init.

Init also lets you privately record interest in a specific person you already know, across six categories: Hangout, Date, Love, Marriage, Apology, and Crush. You may express interest by providing that person's phone number or username.

  • When you express interest in someone, they are not notified.
  • Only when the other person independently expresses interest in you — with at least one overlapping category — are both parties notified of the mutual match.
  • Non-mutual interests are never revealed to any party.

4.3 Post-Match Communication

Once a match is detected — whether through Init Lounge or private interest matching — both users can communicate through an in-app messaging system. Messaging is only available between matched users. Either party may unmatch at any time, which ends the conversation.

4.4 Venues and Lounges

Most venues are pre-listed. If a place is not listed, you may create a lounge for it by providing its name and location. Creating fake, duplicate, or misleading venues is prohibited. A venue owner or authorised representative may request removal of their venue by contacting admin@initmatch.com; we act on takedown requests within 48 hours. Some lounges may be operated as virtual or promotional spaces and are labelled accordingly in the App.
